# AIR_v2

Project AIR: Automated Risk Incident Register, aimed at consolidating different Risk Incidents into a centralised dashboard.
<br><br>

**Team Members:** 

Nelson, Zavier, Emily, Nivi
<br><br>

**AS-IS Workflow:**

-TCC/SAAM/DLM/ORE each maintain their own excel sheet for risk incident tracking. Once a new case comes in, they have to bring their cases forward for discussions, either through verbal discussions or email sending.

-This back and forth of redundant email/verbal conversation can take up to several days.

-Lack of centralised data for all stakeholders, slowing down the time for further actions

-Eg. When a risk incident comes in, TCC/SAAM/DLM/ORE PIC may need to bring forward their details to the Breach PIC for further discussion to see if the case can be classified as a Breach and if it requires an ORE case creation (for BNM Reporting purposes)

**TO-BE:**

-All in one Centralised Dashboard for TCC/SAAM/ORE/DLM PIC to create and update risk cases for further action, eliminating the redundant manual discussions back and forth.

-Editable dashboard which allows further action, such as email trigger to Breach & ORE PIC.

-Google Gemini API for preliminary assessment of Breach.

-Statistics tab to view the number of Potential Breach cases and ORE Reportability cases, available in Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly.
